The system connects case flow, physical meter flow, evidence flow and approval flow in one traceable record.

A case starts with a returned meter and complaint reference. Every system action adds structured evidence or changes a governed state. The case finishes only when technical closure, commercial closure and CAPA closure have each reached their own approved endpoint.

The complete system boundary

Five connected flows become one case record.

The application coordinates the returned meter, enterprise references, laboratory work, diagnostic reasoning and governed decisions. Nothing is passed between disconnected files; every stage reads from and writes back to the same case.

What moves through the system

A physical meter, its digital identity, complaint facts, photographs, genealogy, measurements, hypotheses, approvals, reports, CAPA actions and final disposition.

What the system guarantees

Every transition has prerequisites, an owner, a timestamp, an evidence trail and a defined output. Unsafe, incomplete or unauthorized transitions are blocked.

The logical workflow

Eight states, each with a clear input and output.

The case can only move forward when the current state has produced its required evidence. Exceptions branch to controlled queues and return to the main flow after resolution.

01

Create case

Input: complaint, return and GRN. System: assigns case ID, owner, SLA and custody record. Output: received case.

02

Reconcile identity

Input: reported, printed, optical, SAP, MES and WMS IDs. System: compares all sources. Output: matched identity or exception.

03

Lock evidence

Input: identity, photos, genealogy and custody. System: checks completeness and blocks intervention. Output: test-ready evidence pack.

04

Run safe core tests

Input: approved variant recipe. System: validates safety, fixture and calibration, then records raw results. Output: reproduced symptom and baseline signature.

05

Resolve diagnosis

Input: observations and measurements. System: ranks hypotheses and selects the next discriminating test. Output: evidence-backed causal conclusion.

06

Approve RCA

Input: structured causal chain and evidence links. System: creates internal and customer-safe drafts. Output: approved technical closure.

07

Execute closure

Input: approved RCA and disposition. System: tracks CAPA, repair order, replacement, retention and destruction. Output: independent closure states.

08

Learn across cases

Input: confirmed causes, signatures and batch links. System: clusters recurrence and monitors CAPA. Output: alerts, containment and updated rules.

Non-negotiable orderPreserve → identify → observe → read → energize → test → open → probe → remove components.
Golden case walkthrough

How one blank display becomes a defensible RCA.

The prototype follows fictional case FFR-2026-04782 from intake through agentic diagnosis, approval, CAPA, replacement and population learning.

FFR-2026-04782 · fictional demo caseSP-24-084731 · Display blankKSP-100 single-phase · PCB-24-06-117 · Aravalli Power
01

Identity and context agree

Reported, printed, SAP, MES and WMS identities match. MES genealogy is frozen and the evidence gate marks the meter ready for controlled testing. HES evidence is not required in the initial implementation.

02

The symptom is reproduced

The display remains blank on battery and controlled mains. External inspection shows no impact, burn, moisture or seal anomaly.

03

Core evidence narrows the system

The input path and rectified high-voltage rail are healthy, but low-voltage electronics do not boot and the 3.3 V rail is absent.

Competing hypotheses

Before the next test, the platform keeps four explanations alive.

PSU regulator failure62%
Downstream short21%
MCU failure10%
Battery-only issue7%
Recommended test: measure regulator input/output and downstream rail resistance · 4 minutes · no additional evidence risk.
Result: regulator input 12.1 V; output 0.08 V; downstream resistance 8.4 kΩ. A downstream short is excluded and the regulator-stage failure becomes highly probable.
04

Structured RCA, not narrative-only RCA

Failed function: low-voltage control electronics. Subsystem: power supply. Node: 3.3 V regulator stage. Mechanism: internal regulator failure. Origin remains probable pending batch and supplier analysis.

05

The individual case triggers prevention

A CAPA screens 1,248 potentially affected meters from the PCB batch, preserves regulator samples, reviews the supplier and evaluates an enhanced PSU signature / powered-soak test.

The application components

Nine modules connected by one case state machine.

These are not separate applications. Each module reads the same typed case entities and advances a specific part of the end-to-end workflow. Open any module to see its input, processing logic, output and handoff.

01

Operations command centre

Controls the flow of work across cases, people, stations and integrations.

  • Receives: live case states, SLA clocks, station queues, equipment status and alerts.
  • Works by: aggregating events into inflow, WIP, ageing, turnaround and utilization views.
  • Produces: prioritized queues, bottleneck warnings and systemic alerts.
  • Hands off: the selected case or intervention directly to the responsible workspace.
02

Cases and intake

Creates the digital case and decides whether the return is ready to enter diagnosis.

  • Receives: complaint facts, photographs, GRN/SAP reference, physical receipt and custody events.
  • Works by: reconciling reported, printed, optical, SAP, MES and WMS identities against required evidence rules.
  • Produces: a unique case, owner, SLA, evidence score and ready or exception state.
  • Hands off: ready cases to Case 360; mismatches and missing evidence to controlled exception queues.
03

Case 360 workspace

Acts as the single source of truth throughout the complete case lifecycle.

  • Receives: complaint, identity, genealogy, DLMS, photographs, lab results, approvals and commercial events.
  • Works by: preserving every artifact in a timeline and evaluating gates before energization, teardown, release or destruction.
  • Produces: the current evidence graph, audit trail and independent technical, commercial and CAPA states.
  • Hands off: approved work to the laboratory, diagnostic, reporting or closure module; HES evidence is added here later.
04

Laboratory line

Turns approved test instructions into safe, repeatable and traceable measurements.

  • Receives: meter variant, complaint branch, safety state, recommended route and station availability.
  • Works by: validating operator, fixture, recipe, equipment and calibration before each controlled test.
  • Produces: immutable raw measurements, pass/fail results, observations, stop events and station timestamps.
  • Hands off: results to the diagnostic engine and the meter to the next approved physical station.
05

Agentic diagnosis

Chooses the smallest safe test sequence that can separate competing causes.

  • Receives: complaint, preserved evidence, measurements, variant rules, golden references and similar cases.
  • Works by: ranking hypotheses, recording support and contradiction, then scoring candidate tests by information gain, time, queue and risk.
  • Produces: an explainable next-best-test recommendation and updated hypothesis confidence after every result.
  • Hands off: approved tests to the lab; sufficiently resolved evidence to structured RCA synthesis.
06

RCA, reports and approvals

Converts resolved evidence into governed conclusions and controlled communications.

  • Receives: reproduced symptom, causal evidence, hypothesis history, component finding and confidence.
  • Works by: filling the causal hierarchy and applying technical, quality, liability and customer-release approval gates.
  • Produces: one approved structured record plus internal RCA and redacted customer-safe PDF/DOCX views.
  • Hands off: confirmed causes to CAPA and population intelligence; release status to commercial closure.
07

CAPA management

Turns an approved root cause into owned action and measured prevention.

  • Receives: confirmed cause, affected population, containment need and approval conditions.
  • Works by: tracking containment, correction, corrective action, preventive action, owners, due dates and implementation evidence.
  • Produces: an approved CAPA plan with baseline, target, observation window and effectiveness rule.
  • Hands off: effectiveness results to case closure, governance and recurrence monitoring; failed CAPA reopens action.
08

Batch and population intelligence

Connects individual cases to wider product, supplier and manufacturing patterns.

  • Receives: case signatures, model, PCB batch, firmware, line, project, mechanism and CAPA links.
  • Works by: clustering similar cases, measuring homogeneity, selecting representatives and isolating outliers.
  • Produces: affected-population estimates, master RCA candidates, systemic alerts and containment recommendations.
  • Hands off: patterns to Quality, CAPA, MES feedback and knowledge-rule updates.
09

Knowledge and governance

Defines the rules, permissions and versioned knowledge used by every other module.

  • Receives: approved test methods, safety rules, role policy, golden references, overrides and model performance.
  • Works by: versioning rules, restricting agent tools, auditing changes and monitoring confidence calibration and drift.
  • Produces: publishable test catalogues, permission matrices, model versions, audit records and rollback points.
  • Hands off: approved rules to all modules; the HES adapter and evidence policy are introduced only in a later phase.

Physical and digital operating system

Each external system contributes a defined evidence contract.

The core platform does not copy entire enterprise systems. Simulated adapters request only the required fields, reconcile them to the case identity, retain source references and expose partial or unavailable states. Production adapters will follow the same contract.

SAPReturn and commercial context

Read: GRN, material and customer. Match: case and meter identity. Write later: approved repair-order and replacement status.

MESManufacturing genealogy

Read: BOM, PCB batch, firmware, line tests and rework. Match: manufactured serial. Return: confirmed failure and CAPA feedback.

WMSPhysical traceability

Read: receipt, batch and location. Match: custody event. Write later: quarantine, retention, movement and destruction status.

HES · LATERFuture field chronology

Later read: communication, event, relay and outage history. Align: timestamps to removal. Use: strengthen or contradict lab hypotheses.

DLMS / OPTICALMeter-side evidence

Read: immutable first extraction. Preserve: raw trace and errors. Decode: approved objects without reset or configuration write.

How success will be measured

Operational, diagnostic and preventive intelligence.

The mature system is judged by turnaround time, correctness and recurrence—not by the number of AI features implemented.

Operations

Can the facility process 300 meters per day safely?

  • Inflow, completions and WIP
  • Queue and cycle time by station
  • Median and P95 RCA TAT
  • Equipment utilization and downtime

Quality

Are conclusions correct and systemic patterns found?

  • Failure modes by model and PCB batch
  • Confirmed, inconclusive and NFR rates
  • Later-overturned RCA rate
  • CAPA recurrence and effectiveness

Agent performance

Does automation save work without hiding errors?

  • Recommendation acceptance
  • Tests and analyst minutes saved
  • Class-level precision and recall
  • Confidence calibration and drift

Customer / project

What is happening by utility, AMISP and deployment?

  • Return volume and complaint mix
  • Turnaround and replacement status
  • Confirmed origins and open CAPAs
  • Repeat project-level patterns
Lower RCA turnaround timeFewer waits, fewer unnecessary tests and faster evidence acquisition.
Higher root-cause correctnessStructured evidence, competing hypotheses and controlled validation.
